1. Introduction

City Lighthouse Church ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website and use our communication channels. Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), we act as the Data Controller for the informational processing assets running on this domain.

2. Data We Collect

When you fill out our interactive contact forms or connect with our administrative layers, you voluntarily choose to feed the following personal vectors into our tracking registers:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (Optional)
  • Any specific prayer vectors or localized neighborhood requests you voluntarily place inside the open message field wrapper.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data purely for organizational communication workflows. Your data is isolated strictly to perform the following loops:

  • Processing and responding directly to your specific administrative inquiries or ministry questions.
  • Mapping localized prayer details safely to pastoral care workers if requested.

Warning Safeguard: We strictly do not sell, leak, lease, lease-out, or distribute your logged email strings to third-party data collection companies or programmatic marketing tracking engines.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your coordinates under the regulatory banner of Legitimate Interests (responding to your direct requests) and Consent (granted dynamically when you explicitly click the "Submit Message Form" button block on our tracking interfaces).

5. Data Retention Parameters

We maintain database assets containing your interaction records only for as long as necessary to complete the engagement cycle or to verify secure operational compliance with localized UK non-profit data tracking policies.

6. Your UK Legal Rights

Under UK legal mandates, you retain absolute authority over your records. You can fire any of these commands directly at our administrative office at any time:

The Right to Access

Request comprehensive raw text records of every data variable we hold bound to your profile identity.

The Right to Correct

Instantly update corrupted or out-of-date records such as phone strings or typos in names.

The Right to Erasure

Instruct full deletion parameters to wipe your form logs from our mail handling records permanently.
