The Gathering Framework

What to Expect at Sunday Worship

We intentionally protect our Sunday afternoons from casual distractions. When you step into our physical sanctuary space, you are entering an environment explicitly curated to honor God's presence, lift up passionate praises, and establish uncompromised scriptural clarity.

From heartfelt communion elements to comprehensive line-by-line Bible teaching, everything serves to steady your foundational faith for the week ahead.

Please Note: Our Sunday service experiences are currently gathered and shared in the Tamil Language.
Order of Service Flow
01
Opening Prayer

Aligning our intentions, centering our thoughts, and inviting the presence of God into the house.

02
Praise and Worship

An extended, passionate window of unfeigned praise and singing to lift high the name of Jesus Christ.

03
Congregational Prayer

Standing together as a family to bring localized intercessions and neighborhood needs before the altar.

04
Sermon & Word

Clear, robust, and uncompromising exposition of the scriptures targeting real practical application.

05
Altar Call & Benediction

An open window for personal prayer reflection at the altar, followed by the structural benediction releasing God's blessing over your week.
